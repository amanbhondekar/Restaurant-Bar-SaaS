## WHAT THE CODE DOES TODAY

- A bill carries itemised tax rows: `{label, rate_percent, taxable_amount, amount}`. The default is **CGST 2.5% + SGST 2.5%** (`DEFAULT_TAX_RULES`), used whenever the tenant has no tax config.
- A tenant may have its own rules (`tax_rules`, `settings.tax` or `tax` on the hub's config), each scoped to `all`, `veg`, `non_veg` or `category:<id>`.
- Discounts and the service charge are applied before tax: `taxable_base` is the discounted subtotal plus service charge, and scoped rules share it proportionally.
- Invoice numbers are `INV-` plus six digits, per tenant, derived from the highest number used (so a gap never causes a reuse), and a unique index on `(restaurant_id, invoice_number)` backs it in the cloud. A voided bill keeps its number.
- The receipt prints the restaurant's name, city and phone, the invoice number, items, one line per discount, the service charge and each tax row, and a grand total.

## OTHER FINDINGS

- **Nothing sets the tax config.** No file under `src/admin/` reads or writes `tax_rules` or `service_charge_percent`, and the `restaurants` table has a `settings` JSONB but no tax column. In practice every tenant gets CGST 2.5% + SGST 2.5% unless someone edits the hub's config by hand. I did not trace whether pairing copies `restaurants.settings` into that config.
- **The printed bill does not add up exactly.** Each tax row is rounded to two places, and `grand_total` is the rupee-rounded sum (`Math.round`), but the receipt has no round-off line (no round-off handling exists anywhere in `hub_server/lib`). The printed rows and the GRAND TOTAL can differ by up to ₹0.50.
- **Prices are treated as tax-exclusive.** Tax is added on top of `qty × price`. There is no tax-inclusive pricing mode.
- **Split bills** divide `grand_total` into shares; a share has an amount and a payment state but no tax breakdown of its own.

## QUESTIONS FOR THE CA (feeds decision D5, and the fix)

1. What goes on a restaurant's bill: supplier GSTIN, SAC, rate, and whether a place of supply is needed for dine-in?
2. Must the invoice series reset each financial year, and in what format (prefix, year, separator) within 16 characters?
3. Is the service charge part of the taxable value? (The code includes it.)
4. How should bar items be taxed? (A `category:` scoped rule can express an exemption, but there is no editor to set it.)
5. Is a credit note needed when a paid bill is refunded, and what must it carry?
6. Is the ₹0.50 rupee rounding acceptable, and should it print as a round-off line?
7. What does Kullina itself need on its own invoices to restaurants (Phase 5-4)? That is the same question for a different issuer.
